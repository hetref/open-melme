---
title: Assigning Aliases to Mailboxes
description: Learn how to link multiple custom email addresses to a single mailbox container.
---

## 1. Overview

Linking an alias to a mailbox is how you direct incoming emails into your secure storage. Once an alias is assigned to a mailbox, all mail sent to that address will bypass forwarding and land directly inside that mailbox.

---

## 2. Why This Matters

By assigning multiple aliases to a single mailbox, you can consolidate different email streams. For example, you can have `hello@yourcompany.com`, `info@yourcompany.com`, and `office@yourcompany.com` all route to the same "General Queries" mailbox, saving you from having to check multiple inboxes.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### Step-by-Step Assignment:
1. Go to the **Aliases** tab in the Admin Panel.
2. Click **Create Alias** (or click **Edit** on an existing alias).
3. Under **Mode**, select **Mailbox**.
4. In the **Mailbox** dropdown field, choose the specific Mailbox you want to link.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

Any incoming email sent to that alias is now routed to the selected Mailbox in real-time.

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Leaving the Alias in Forward Mode:** If you create a mailbox, but forget to switch your alias's Mode from "Forward" to "Mailbox," your emails will continue to be forwarded to your personal email, and the MelMe mailbox will remain empty.
* **Orphaned Aliases:** If you delete a mailbox, any aliases linked to it will lose their target destination and stop working. Always update your aliases' routing before deleting a mailbox!

---

## 5. Tips

<Callout type="info">
  **Domain Freedom:** You can assign aliases from Domain A (e.g. `info@domainA.com`) and Domain B (e.g. `info@domainB.com`) to the exact same Mailbox. This is incredibly helpful for managing multiple sister companies or brand projects together!
</Callout>
