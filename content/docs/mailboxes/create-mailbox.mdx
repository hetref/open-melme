## 1. Overview

Deploying a new mailbox container allows you to establish a secure, isolated workspace to receive and send emails under your domain brands.

---

## 2. Why This Matters

Properly setting up a mailbox's name, slug, and sender name ensures a professional customer experience and clear internal organization as you scale your email infrastructure.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### Step-by-Step Mailbox Creation:
1. Log in to the Admin Dashboard and select **Mailboxes** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Create Mailbox** in the top right corner.
3. Complete the form fields:
   * **Mailbox Name:** A clean internal title for identification (e.g., "Main Helpdesk").
   * **Mailbox Slug:** A unique URL-friendly word (using only lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens). This is the username for logging into the mailbox (e.g. `helpdesk`).
   * **Sender Name:** The display name recipients see when they get an email from you (e.g., "MelMe Help Desk").
   * **Personal Email (Optional):** Reference email for administrative or password recovery purposes.
   * **Description (Optional):** Internal notes about who has access or the purpose of the mailbox.
   * **Mailbox Password:** Set a secure password for this specific container.
4. Click **Create Mailbox**. The container is immediately active!

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Choosing a duplicate slug:** Slugs must be completely unique across your entire MelMe workspace. If `support` is taken, try `support-desk` or `support-inbox`.
* **Not recording the credentials:** Make sure to note down the **Mailbox Slug** and **Password** securely (such as in a password manager). You will need these to log in to the **My Mailbox** app.

---

## 5. Tips

<Callout type="info">
  **Security Recommendation:** Always use unique passwords for each mailbox you create, especially if you are sharing access with different team members. Do not reuse your primary admin password!
</Callout>
