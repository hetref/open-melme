## 1. Overview

Each mailbox can be customized to match your branding and team structure. You can edit these details at any time from your Admin Panel.

---

## 2. Why This Matters

Accurate mailbox settings are key to presenting a professional brand to your recipients and keeping your admin dashboard organized as you add more mailboxes.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### Configuration Options:
Go to the **Mailboxes** tab in the Admin Panel and click **Edit** on a mailbox to customize:

### 1. Sender Name
This is the name display that recipients will see in their inboxes (e.g. "Support Desk" or "Alex from MelMe"). Keep this clean and recognizable.

### 2. Mailbox Description
An internal note explaining the purpose of the mailbox or who has access (e.g., "Main workspace for the customer service team").

### 3. Mailbox Tags
You can add tags (like `Sales`, `Internal`, `Urgent`) to organize and filter your mailboxes within the Admin Dashboard.

### 4. Active Toggle
If you want to temporarily suspend a mailbox's activity (blocking all logins and routing) without permanently deleting it, you can toggle its **Active** switch off.

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Leaving the Sender Name blank or generic:** If you don't configure a clear Sender Name, your outgoing emails might show up in recipients' inboxes as sent by an anonymous sender, which reduces open rates.
* **Using confusing slugs:** Keep your slugs descriptive (e.g., `sales-inbox` instead of `box123`) so you and your team can easily remember how to log in.

---

## 5. Tips

<Callout type="info">
  **Branding Recommendation:** Set your Sender Name as `[Your Name] | [Company Name]` (e.g., `David | MelMe Support`) for a warm, professional, yet personal touch.
</Callout>
