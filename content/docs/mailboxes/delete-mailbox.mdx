title: Deleting a Mailbox
description: Step-by-step instructions on how to permanently delete a mailbox container and understand its impact on your emails.
---

## 1. Overview

If you no longer need an isolated mailbox container, you can delete it from your Admin Dashboard. 

---

## 2. Why This Matters

<Callout type="warning">
  **Critical Warning:** Deleting a mailbox is a permanent, irreversible action. Once deleted, all emails, threads, sent logs, and file attachments stored inside that mailbox will be permanently erased from MelMe's database.
</Callout>

Understanding the impact of mailbox deletion protects your business from accidental data loss.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### Step 1: Pre-Deletion Checklist
Before clicking delete, ensure you have:
* Saved or exported any important customer email threads.
* Downloaded any critical file attachments.
* Re-routed any active aliases pointing to this mailbox (otherwise, emails sent to those aliases will fail to deliver).

### Step 2: Delete the Mailbox in MelMe
1. Log in to the Admin Panel and select **Mailboxes**.
2. Locate the mailbox you wish to delete.
3. Click the **Delete** button (trash can icon).
4. Review the confirmation warnings and confirm your action. 

The mailbox and its contents are immediately deleted, and any active logged-in sessions on the **My Mailbox** app will be instantly terminated.

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Leaving active aliases pointing to the deleted mailbox:** If you forget to update or delete the aliases that were routed to the mailbox, they will become "orphaned" and bounce all incoming emails.
* **Accidental Deletion:** Ensure you select the correct mailbox in the dashboard to avoid deleting important customer or billing logs.

---

## 5. Tips

<Callout type="info">
  **Suspension Alternative:** If you just want to stop users from logging into a mailbox or temporarily block incoming messages without losing your historical email data, toggle the mailbox **Active** switch to "Inactive" instead of deleting it!
</Callout>
