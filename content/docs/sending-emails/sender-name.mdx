title: Setting Your Sender Name
description: Learn how to manage the display name recipients see when they receive emails from your mailbox.
---

## 1. Overview

Your **Sender Name** is the display name that appears next to your email address in the recipient's inbox (e.g. showing "John Doe" instead of just `john@yourdomain.com`). You can set and update this display name directly within your mailbox settings.

---

## 2. Why This Matters

An email from a blank or anonymous sender looks highly suspicious and reduces open rates. A professional, clear Sender Name builds immediate trust, improves open rates, and makes your communication immediately recognizable to your customers.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### Setting the Sender Name:
1. Log in to the **My Mailbox** app.
2. Click **Settings** in the left sidebar menu.
3. Locate the **Sender Name** input field.
4. Enter your professional display name:
   - For individual business: `Sarah Jenkins`
   - For company department: `MelMe Billing Team`
   - For a combined feel: `David | MelMe Support`
5. Click **Save Changes**. All outgoing emails sent after this update will use the new display name.

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Leaving it default or blank:** If left unconfigured, some mail servers will display your email slug or leave the name field empty, which looks generic.
* **Using overly long titles:** Keep your display name under 30 characters. Long names can get cut off by email clients, especially on mobile devices.

---

## 5. Tips

<Callout type="info">
  **Consistency Recommendation:** Maintain consistency across your team. For example, if multiple employees use different mailboxes, have them all use a standard format like `[Name] at [Company]` (e.g. `David at Acme`).
</Callout>
