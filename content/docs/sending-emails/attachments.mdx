## 1. Overview

When composing emails, you can attach files—such as images, PDF invoices, word documents, or slides—to share with your recipients. 

---

## 2. Why This Matters

Sharing quotes, invoices, agreements, and spreadsheets is a vital part of running any business. MelMe makes attaching these files secure and simple, ensuring they load quickly for your recipients.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### Step-by-Step Attachment Guide:
1. Open **My Mailbox** and click **Compose** to draft a new email.
2. At the bottom of the composer, click the **Attachment** (paperclip) icon.
3. Select the file(s) from your computer or mobile device.
4. **Upload Status:** You will see a progress bar indicating that the file is being securely uploaded. Once uploaded, a list of attached files will display at the bottom of the composer.
5. Click **Send** to deliver your message along with the attached files.

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Exceeding standard email size limits:** Most email servers (like Gmail or Exchange) will block incoming emails that are larger than **10MB**. To guarantee successful delivery, keep your total attachment size below 10MB per email.
* **Attaching dangerous file types:** MelMe blocks suspicious executable file formats (like `.exe`, `.bat`, or `.zip` files containing code) for security reasons. Stick to standard formats like PDFs, JPGs, PNGs, and Microsoft Office documents.

---

## 5. Tips

<Callout type="info">
  **Sending Large Files:** If you need to send files larger than 10MB (like high-res videos or huge project folders), upload them to a cloud sharing service (like Google Drive or Dropbox) and paste the link in your email body instead!
</Callout>
