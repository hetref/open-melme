## 1. Overview

Sending outbound emails is one of the core features of MelMe. Using the **My Mailbox** app, you can draft clean, professional emails and send them to any recipient on the internet using your custom domain addresses.

---

## 2. Why This Matters

To maintain a professional business identity, you need to communicate under your custom domain brand (e.g. `alex@mycompany.com`). Sending emails directly from MelMe ensures that recipients see your official brand identity rather than a personal Gmail or Outlook address.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### Steps to Send an Email:
1. Log in to your secure **My Mailbox** panel.
2. Click the **Compose** button.
3. **Choose Your Sender Address:** Use the **From** dropdown menu to select which active alias you want to send from. You can only choose aliases linked to this mailbox.
4. **Specify Recipients:** Type the destination address in the **To** field.
5. **Add Subject & Body:** Enter a descriptive subject line and draft your message in the email composer.
6. Click **Send**. The message is immediately processed and delivered to the recipient.

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Sending from an Unverified Domain Alias:** Ensure your domain is fully verified in the Admin Panel before trying to send emails. If the domain is pending, outbound emails will fail.
* **Forgetting to check the "From" field:** If you manage multiple aliases in one mailbox, failing to verify the From selection can result in sending department-related emails from the wrong address (e.g. sending sales responses from `support@`).
