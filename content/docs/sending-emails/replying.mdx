## 1. Overview

Replying to incoming messages is simple inside MelMe. Clicking **Reply** on an email thread sets up an automatic composer that preserves your conversation context and matches your sender identity perfectly.

---

## 2. Why This Matters

When clients engage with your business, they expect a single continuous thread rather than disjointed emails. Keeping replies grouped together helps track issues, resolve questions, and keep your interactions organized.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### Replying Step-by-Step:
1. Open your mailbox and select a conversation thread in the **Inbox**.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the reading panel and click **Reply**.
3. **Smart Identity Matching:** MelMe reads the incoming message headers and automatically sets the **From** field to the exact alias that received the original message.
4. Type your reply in the composer.
5. Click **Send**. Your reply is delivered and instantly appended to the bottom of the conversation thread.

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Replying from an Unrelated Mailbox Alias:** If a customer wrote to `billing@yourdomain.com`, ensure you reply from `billing@yourdomain.com` rather than switching it to `sales@yourdomain.com` unless absolutely necessary.
* **Creating a New Message Instead of Replying:** If you compose a fresh email to reply to a customer, it will create a brand new thread instead of linking to the old one. Always use the **Reply** button on the thread to keep conversations grouped!

---

## 5. Tips

<Callout type="info">
  **Automatic Quotes:** MelMe automatically appends the previous conversation history below your reply, helping recipients remember what was discussed.
</Callout>
