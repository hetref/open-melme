## 1. Overview

The **Inbox** is the primary landing area inside MelMe's **My Mailbox** app. This is where all incoming emails sent to the aliases connected to this specific mailbox are safely gathered and displayed in a clean, real-time list.

---

## 2. Why This Matters

An organized, real-time inbox ensures you never miss a critical client inquiry or transaction email. Having a clear visual distinction between read and unread messages helps you prioritize your daily communication workflows.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### Navigating the Inbox:
1. Go to the **My Mailbox** login page and log in using your mailbox slug and password.
2. You will be automatically routed to the **Inbox** tab.
3. **The Email List:** The left panel displays a vertical feed of all received messages:
   - **Unread Messages:** Highlighted with a distinct visual indicator (e.g. blue dot) to grab your attention.
   - **Sender Details:** Displays the sender's email, subject line, and a short snippet of the message body.
4. **Reading an Email:** Click on any email in the list to load the full, formatted HTML message and its conversation history in the right reading panel.

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Logging in with an administrator email:** Remember, the **My Mailbox** app is specifically for individual inboxes. You must log in using your mailbox **Slug** (e.g. `support-inbox`) and the mailbox password.
* **Confusing Inbox with Spam:** If a verified alias doesn't receive a test email immediately, check the Admin Panel logs to verify if it was received by MelMe.

---

## 5. Tips

<Callout type="info">
  **Real-Time Sync:** The inbox updates dynamically. When a new email arrives on your domain, it will automatically appear in your inbox list without requiring you to manually refresh the page!
</Callout>
