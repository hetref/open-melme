## 1. Overview

Users logged in to the **My Mailbox** app have access to a dedicated **Settings** panel. From here, you can manage your personal display options and update your password without requiring access to the main Admin Dashboard.

---

## 2. Why This Matters

Allowing individual mailbox users to manage their own settings and passwords increases efficiency, promotes security best practices, and reduces the administrative burden on the account owner.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### Accessing Settings:
1. Log in to your mailbox using the **My Mailbox** page.
2. Click **Settings** in the left navigation menu.

### Settings Options Available:

### 1. Update Display Name / Sender Name
Change how your name appears to external recipients when you send an email. Enter your name in the input field and click **Save**.

### 2. Reset Mailbox Password
To update your mailbox security key:
1. Navigate to the **Reset Password** tab inside the settings panel.
2. Enter your **Current Password**.
3. Type in your **New Password** and confirm it.
4. Click **Update Password**. The new password will take effect immediately.

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Updating the admin password instead of the mailbox password:** Changing your password here only affects this specific mailbox. It does not change your primary admin account login.
* **Choosing a weak password:** When resetting your mailbox password, ensure it is robust and hard to guess.

---

## 5. Tips

<Callout type="warning">
  **Security Note:** If you change your password, any other active sessions logged into this mailbox (such as on an employee's browser) will be logged out automatically.
</Callout>
