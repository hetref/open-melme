## 1. Overview

Replying to incoming messages inside **My Mailbox** is designed to be seamless. When you click reply on a thread, MelMe automatically sets up the response to maintain a professional, uninterrupted conversation.

---

## 2. Why This Matters

When a customer writes to `billing@yourdomain.com`, they expect the reply to come from `billing@yourdomain.com`—not `support@yourdomain.com` or your personal Gmail. MelMe ensures you reply from the exact address the recipient wrote to, preserving your brand credibility.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### Replying to a Thread:
1. Open your **Inbox** in **My Mailbox**.
2. Select the email conversation thread you want to reply to.
3. Click the **Reply** button at the bottom of the reading panel.
4. **Automatic Matching:** MelMe reads the conversation headers and automatically sets the **From** field to the exact alias address that received the original email.
5. Type your reply in the rich text area.
6. Click **Send**. The reply will be sent out, and the conversation thread will update to show your outgoing message at the bottom.

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Overriding the From Address accidentally:** If you have multiple aliases routed to the mailbox, you *can* manually change the **From** dropdown when replying. However, doing so can confuse the recipient if they suddenly receive a reply from a different address than they wrote to. Stick to the automatically selected address!
* **Leaving the compose editor open:** If you navigate away without clicking Send, your draft reply will not be saved automatically. Always click Send or copy your text before switching pages.
