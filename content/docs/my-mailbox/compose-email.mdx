## 1. Overview

Composing a new email is simple inside **My Mailbox**. You can start fresh conversations with any recipient on the web using any of the custom aliases assigned to your mailbox.

---

## 2. Why This Matters

Drafting clean, structured messages directly under your professional brand establishes authority and makes it easy to proactively reach out to new clients, vendors, or team members.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### Step-by-Step Composing:
1. Log in to **My Mailbox** and click the **Compose** button in the sidebar.
2. **Select the "From" Alias:** 
   - If your mailbox has multiple aliases assigned to it (e.g. `sales@brand.com` and `support@brand.com`), select which identity you want to write from using the **From** dropdown menu.
3. **Specify the Recipient ("To"):** Enter the recipient's email address in the **To** field.
4. **Enter a Subject:** Write a clear, brief subject line.
5. **Write the Message Body:** Use the built-in text editor to draft your email message.
6. **Add Attachments:** If needed, click the paperclip icon to upload files.
7. Click **Send**. Your email is instantly routed and delivered.

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Selecting the wrong "From" alias:** If you manage multiple businesses or departments in one mailbox, always double-check the **From** dropdown to ensure you are writing from the correct address.
* **Adding too many attachments at once:** Large files can take a few moments to process. Upload files one at a time to ensure smooth delivery.

---

## 5. Tips

<Callout type="info">
  **Sender Name Consistency:** The sender name that recipients see (e.g. "Sarah Jenkins") is defined in your Mailbox Settings. Ensure this is up to date before composing!
</Callout>
