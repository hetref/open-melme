## 1. Overview

Whenever you log in to MelMe or a mailbox, a secure **Session** is created. MelMe actively monitors these sessions to keep track of which devices, IP addresses, and browsers are currently logged in to your account.

---

## 2. Why This Matters

Device session tracking gives you full visibility over active connections:
* **Unauthorized Access Detection:** If you notice a login session from an unrecognized device or country, you can flag it immediately.
* **Instant Revocation:** You can log out stale or compromised devices instantly, cutting off access in real-time.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### Checking Active Sessions:
1. Log in to your Admin Dashboard.
2. Go to the **Profile** or **Security** page.
3. Locate the **Active Sessions** list.
4. You will see a list of all logged-in browsers, showing:
   - **Device Type & Browser:** (e.g. "Chrome on macOS").
   - **IP Address & Location:** The digital address of the device.
   - **Last Active Time:** When the device last loaded a page.
5. **Logging Out a Session:** Click **Revoke** or **Log Out** next to any session in the list to immediately kick that device off your account.

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Leaving sessions open on public devices:** If you log in to your account on a shared office computer or library device, always click **Sign Out** before leaving. Relying on auto-logout timers exposes your account to risk.
* **Confusing your own mobile browser session:** If you log in from your phone, it might list a different IP address (depending on your mobile carrier). Do not panic and revoke it unless the location is highly suspicious!

---

## 5. Tips

<Callout type="info">
  **Automatic Expiration:** For your safety, idle login sessions will automatically expire and require a re-login after a designated period of inactivity.
</Callout>
