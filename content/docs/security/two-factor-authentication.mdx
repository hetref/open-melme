## 1. Overview

**Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** adds a vital second layer of defense to your Admin Account. When enabled, logging in requires two things: something you know (your password) and something you have (a dynamic code generated on your mobile phone).

---

## 2. Why This Matters

Passwords can be guessed, phished, or stolen in database leaks from other sites. 2FA ensures that even if a malicious actor acquires your password, they cannot access your MelMe account because they do not have your physical mobile phone.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### Step-by-Step Setup:
1. Log in to your MelMe Admin Panel.
2. Click on **Profile** in the sidebar.
3. Locate the **Two-Factor Authentication** section.
4. Click **Enable 2FA**.
5. A QR code will display on the screen along with a text key.
6. Open your favorite authenticator app on your phone (such as **Google Authenticator**, **Microsoft Authenticator**, or **Bitwarden**).
7. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera.
8. Enter the 6-digit verification code generated by your app into MelMe to verify the setup.
9. **Save Backup Codes:** MelMe will display a list of emergency backup codes. Copy and save them in a highly secure place.

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Losing access to your phone without backups:** If you lose your phone, delete the authenticator app, or buy a new phone without migrating your keys, you won't be able to log in. Keep your backup codes safe to prevent permanent lockouts!
* **Sharing 2FA QR Codes:** Never screenshot or share your 2FA setup QR code with anyone.

---

## 5. Tips

<Callout type="info">
  **Multiple Authenticator Apps:** You can scan the same QR code on multiple devices (like your phone and your tablet) during the initial setup if you want a backup authenticator device!
</Callout>
