## 1. Overview

Once your aliases are set up, you can manage, filter, and monitor them directly from the main **Aliases** page. MelMe makes it simple to change where an alias points, check delivery histories, and keep your workspace tidy.

---

## 2. Why This Matters

As your business or personal workflows evolve, you may need to update your email routes—such as changing who handles billing questions or pointing a contact address to a new mailbox. Knowing how to efficiently manage your aliases prevents configuration downtime.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### 1. Editing Routing Destinations
If you need to change a forward destination or assign an alias to a different mailbox:
1. Go to the **Aliases** tab.
2. Locate your alias and click the **Edit** button (pencil icon).
3. Switch the mode or select a different destination/mailbox.
4. Click **Save Changes**. The routing rules are updated instantly in real-time.

### 2. Monitoring Email Logs
If you want to verify if an alias is receiving emails:
1. Go to the Admin Dashboard's main log or click **Logs** next to your domain.
2. You can view all email activities associated with each alias, including whether they were successfully forwarded, routed, or if an error occurred.

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Changing routing during a critical conversation:** If you switch an alias from Mailbox mode to Forward mode mid-conversation, future messages will be forwarded and will no longer show up in the mailbox thread history. Plan your routing updates during low-activity periods!
* **Mismatched domains:** Ensure you are editing the correct alias if you have similar names across different domains (e.g., `contact@brandA.com` vs `contact@brandB.com`).

---

## 5. Tips

<Callout type="info">
  **Organized Search:** Use the search bar at the top of the Aliases page to quickly find specific aliases by name or domain, saving you time as your list of addresses grows.
</Callout>
