---
title: Activating and Deactivating Aliases
description: Learn how to temporarily turn your email aliases on or off with a single click.
---

## 1. Overview

Every alias in MelMe has a status toggle: **Active** or **Inactive**. When an alias is deactivated, it remains in your dashboard list, but our servers will immediately reject any new email sent to that address.

---

## 2. Why This Matters

Sometimes, you need to stop receiving emails from a specific address, but you don't want to permanently delete the alias:
* **Spam Control:** If an alias you registered on a shopping site starts getting flooded with junk mail, you can toggle it to "Inactive" to instantly block the spam.
* **Temporary Campaigns:** If you run a marketing campaign with an address like `promo-july@yourdomain.com`, you can deactivate it once the month is over.
* **Security:** Quickly disable an address if you suspect it has been compromised, without losing its historical settings.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### Step-by-Step Toggle:
1. Log in to the MelMe Admin Panel and click **Aliases**.
2. Locate the alias you wish to change in the list.
3. Find the **Active Status** switch next to the alias name.
4. Click the switch:
   - **Green / Toggled On:** The alias is **Active** and routing emails.
   - **Grey / Toggled Off:** The alias is **Inactive**. Any emails sent to this address will bounce back to the sender as undeliverable.

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Forgetting that deactivation causes bounces:** If a customer or client tries to write to an inactive alias, they will receive a "mailbox unavailable" bounce email. Make sure you don't accidentally deactivate important active business lines!
* **Expecting historical emails to disappear:** Deactivating an alias only blocks *new* incoming emails. If the alias is a Mailbox Alias, all historically received emails will remain safe in your mailbox storage.

---

## 5. Tips

<Callout type="warning">
  **Important Note:** If you deactivate a Forward Alias, no emails will be routed to your personal inbox. Always make sure to reactivate it if you are expecting a verification code or reset link!
</Callout>
