## 1. Overview

Adding a new alias allows your custom domain to receive emails under a new username. MelMe allows you to create unlimited aliases for any verified domain on your account.

---

## 2. Why This Matters

Creating aliases dynamically lets you manage multiple projects or department addresses on the fly, instantly adapting your communications as your brand or team grows.

---

## 3. How to Use It

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Log in to the Admin Dashboard and click **Aliases** in the sidebar.
2. Click the **Create Alias** button in the upper right.
3. Fill in the configuration fields:
   * **Local Part:** The text prefix before the `@` symbol (e.g. `billing` for `billing@mycompany.com`).
   * **Domain:** Choose the verified custom domain from the list.
   * **Personal Email (Optional):** Enter a backup email address for reference.
   * **Routing Mode:**
     - **Forward:** Reroutes emails to your own personal inbox.
     - **Mailbox:** Delivers emails to a secure, isolated MelMe inbox container.
   * **Destination / Mailbox:** Based on your routing mode, enter the destination email or select your target mailbox from the list.
4. Click **Save** or **Create Alias**. The alias is immediately active!

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Creating duplicate local parts:** You cannot have two aliases named `hello@mybrand.com`. Each local part for a specific domain must be completely unique.
* **Adding special characters:** Avoid using special characters (like spaces or brackets) in the local part of your alias. Stick to letters, numbers, hyphens, and periods (e.g., `john.doe` or `billing-queries`).

---

## 5. Tips

<Callout type="info">
  **Active Toggle:** All aliases are created as **Active** by default. You can temporarily turn off any alias from the aliases list using the active switch, without deleting the configuration.
</Callout>
