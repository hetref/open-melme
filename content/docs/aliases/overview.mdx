## 1. Overview

An **Alias** is a virtual email address built using your custom domain (e.g., `support@yourcompany.com`, `sales@yourcompany.com`, or `hello@yourcompany.com`). 

Unlike a traditional email inbox, an alias doesn't have a login or permanent storage of its own. Instead, it acts as a smart router, immediately catching any incoming email sent to that address and forwarding or storing it according to your configurations.

---

## 2. Why This Matters

Creating aliases offers massive benefits for both business and personal use:
* **Professional Image:** Set up separate addresses for different purposes (e.g., `invoices@`, `jobs@`, `newsletter@`) to look like a fully structured business from day one.
* **Inbox Security:** Stop giving your primary personal email address to online shopping sites or services. Instead, generate a specific alias for each site. If a site leaks your email or starts spamming you, just turn off the alias!
* **Clean Organization:** Route different business functions to the appropriate team members or folders effortlessly.

---

## 3. How to Use It (Choosing the Right Alias Type)

In MelMe, there are two primary routing modes for aliases:

1. **Forward Aliases:** Incoming emails are redirected instantly to your primary personal address (e.g. `yourname@gmail.com`). 
2. **Mailbox Aliases:** Incoming emails are stored inside a secure MelMe inbox (Mailbox) so you can log in, view, and reply professionally.

---

## 4. Common Mistakes

* **Treating an Alias as a Full Login Account:** Remember, you cannot log in to MelMe's **My Mailbox** app using an alias. You can only log in using a **Mailbox Slug**!
* **Typos in destination addresses:** When setting up a forward alias, double-check that your destination email is spelled perfectly, or you will miss incoming messages.
